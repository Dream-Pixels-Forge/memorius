How to run & use the bookcase

Run: memorius bookcase — opens this page on http://127.0.0.1:8400/bookcase.html (loopback only). Add --no-browser to serve without opening a tab, or --port 8912 --static-port 8400 to change ports.

  • Walk: WASD / arrow keys at eye height — you can't pass through shelves.
  • Look: click-drag to look around (pointer-lock when available).
  • Vault: pick a vault from the dropdown (from GET /vaults).
  • Layout: switch by shelf / by recency to re-arrange cases.
  • Search: type + Enter/Go — the camera flies to the matching book.
  • Book click: opens the detail panel (content + librarian speech if on).
  • Mic: hold to talk (Chromium only).
  • Reset view: click ⟲ view to return to the start.
