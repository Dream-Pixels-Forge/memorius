How to run & use the bookcase

Run: memorius bookcase — opens this page on http://127.0.0.1:8400/bookcase.html (loopback only). Add --no-browser to serve without opening a tab, or --port 8912 --static-port 8400 to change ports.

  • Walk: WASD / arrow keys at eye height, Shift to hurry — you can't pass through shelves.
  • Look: click the room to grab mouse-look (crosshair), Esc to release; or click-drag.
  • Ask: / then type (or hold the mic) — the librarian's lantern flies off and the camera follows it over the stacks to the book; hits slide out of the shelf.
  • Results: N / P walk you to the next / previous match, F flies back to the current one.
  • Read: click a book — it is drawn out and opened; "Read aloud" lets the librarian read it.
  • Map: M toggles the floor plan; click it to be walked there.
  • Index: I (or ☰ index) lists every bay with its book and folder counts — click a bay, Enter on a focused row, and you are walked into it facing the shelves. X or ✦ surprise opens a random book.
  • Floor plan: each shelf is a bay off the main aisle (alternating left / right, signs hang at each opening); each folder is a case inside its bay, read clockwise; books run left → right from eye level down. Positions never move.
  • Vault / layout: switch vault (from GET /vaults); "light newest" makes the newest books glow and lean out — the newest also stand on the table by the doors.
  • Reset: R or ⟲ view. Space/Esc skips a camera move.
  • Colour: each folder has its own binding colour; fresher memories are brighter with shinier gilt.
